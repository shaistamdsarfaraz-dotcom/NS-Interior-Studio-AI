ABOUT NS DESIGN AI

Design Smarter.
Live Better.

NS Design AI is an intelligent interior design assistant that helps you transform your ideas into beautiful, functional, and affordable spaces using the power of AI.

We combine image processing, smart recommendations, and real-world budgeting to give you complete interior design guidance — all in one place.

Design. Your Space. Your Way.

Our Mission

To empower every homeowner and design enthusiast with affordable, AI-powered tools that make interior design decisions easy, personalized, and inspiring.

Our Vision

To become a trusted AI interior design platform that helps people create beautiful, comfortable spaces effortlessly, without needing a professional designer.

Our Values

  • Innovation with purpose

  • Design for everyone
